Manual transcription of Fourteenth Census of the United States (1920), Vol. II Population,
Chapter VI, Table 10 "Distribution of foreign-born population by principal countries of birth,
with rank in 1920, by divisions and states: 1920, 1910, and 1900", pp. 710-726
(file ../41084484v2ch08.pdf, PDF pages 26-42; downloaded from
https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/decennial/1920/volume-2/41084484v2ch08.pdf).

Transcribed by the data-gathering agent from 300-dpi renderings of the scanned pages.  Columns kept:
1920 "Both sexes", 1910, 1900 (the 1920 male/female, per-cent and increase columns were not kept;
the increase columns were used to resolve hard-to-read digits).  One "#STATE" block per state;
lines "label,1920,1910,1900"; NA = no figure printed (country not shown separately that year).
"French" = French Canadians, a sub-line of Canada.

Every state block satisfies: listed countries (excluding the French sub-line) + "All other
countries" = "Total foreign born" for each of the three years, and every total equals the WP81
Table 14 state figure (see ../../../../scripts/stock/build_hist_1920_t10.py).  Digits corrected
during that check (misread first, confirmed on the image and by the increase columns):
Maine Finland 1920 1,393; Connecticut Canada 1920 24,679; Missouri England 1900 15,666;
Kansas Scotland 1910 3,591; Maryland Canada 1910 1,430 and England 1920 5,113; Virginia total
1920 31,705, Portugal 1900 29, Poland 1920 1,103; West Virginia Syria 1910 726; Texas All other
1920 10,760; Utah Switzerland 1920 1,566.
