Manual transcription of U.S. Census of Population 1950, Vol. II Characteristics of the Population,
Part 1 United States Summary, Table 71 "Country of birth of the foreign-born white population, by
regions, divisions, and states: 1950", pages 1-122 (file 21983999v2p1ch3.pdf, PDF page 38: columns
All countries..Hungary) and 1-123 (PDF page 39: Yugoslavia..Not reported).

Transcribed by the data-gathering agent from 300-dpi renderings of the scanned pages, in blocks:
  p<page>_<L|R>_<block>.csv   L = first 9 columns of the page, R = remaining 8 columns;
  block A = United States, regions and divisions; B = New England + Middle Atlantic states;
  C = E.N. Central + W.N. Central; D = South Atlantic; E = E.S. Central + W.S. Central; F = Mountain + Pacific.
A value ending in "?" was poorly printed (almost all in the Hungary column, which is faint in the scan)
and was confirmed or corrected with the table's own sums (state row = All countries; states = division;
divisions = region; regions = United States).  Corrections made that way:
  Indiana/Hungary 6,701 -> 6,703; Delaware/Hungary 231 -> 233; Maryland/Hungary 1,318 -> 1,518;
  District of Columbia/Hungary 336 -> 558; Arizona/Czechoslovakia 855 -> 355 (misread).
After these, every row and column constraint of the table holds exactly (see build_hist_1950_t71.py).
Machine OCR (RapidOCR) of the same pages was tried and was too unreliable to use; it served only as
a rough cross-check during transcription.
